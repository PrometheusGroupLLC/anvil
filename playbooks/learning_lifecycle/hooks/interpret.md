# Interpret Observation

Append the interpretation to `definition.md` while preserving the original observation content.

Add these sections:

## Interpretation

What we believe this observation means.

## Implications

How this affects future work.

## Scope

Under what conditions this applies, and where it does not.

## Supporting evidence

Additional observations that corroborate the interpretation, or `Pending cross-context confirmation` if none yet.

After the interpreted content is present, move the lifecycle toward `conclusion_review` through the engine.
