# Review Conclusion

Review whether an interpreted conclusion is sound enough to advance toward established knowledge.

Check:

- Is the interpretation well-reasoned and supported by the observation?
- Are the implications clear and actionable?
- Is the scope specific — both where it applies and where it does not?
- Is supporting evidence present, or is the pending-confirmation status honest?
- Does the conclusion preserve the original observation content?

Write the review in `review.md`. After satisfied conclusion review, the learning advances to `established`; `definition.md` is frozen and later changes must use the amendment loop.
