# Review Resolved Decision

Review whether a resolved decision is sound enough to become durable institutional knowledge.

Check:

- Is the rationale clear and well-reasoned?
- Were alternatives genuinely considered?
- Are validity conditions specific enough to act on later?
- Is the investigation link accurate when one exists?
- Does the resolution preserve the original tension content?
- Are spawned initiatives, tracks, or decisions identified?

Write the review in `review.md`. After satisfied resolution review, `definition.md` is frozen and later changes must use the amendment loop.
