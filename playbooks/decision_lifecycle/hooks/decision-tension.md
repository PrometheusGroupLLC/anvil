# Review Decision Tension

Review whether a decision in `tension` is a real, useful question.

Check:

- Is the question real and well-scoped?
- Is the context accurate and sufficient?
- Is it distinct from existing decisions?
- Are domain tags useful for discovery?
- Is lineage traceable to the work that surfaced the tension?
- Are related decisions, initiatives, proposals, or tracks linked?

Write the review in `review.md`. Tension review validates the question, not an answer, and does not freeze `definition.md`.
